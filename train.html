🚂 Train Conductor Scheduler

Plan your weekly train conductor schedule. Add your players, set a rule for each day, then click Apply Rules to auto-fill the week. Post the schedule to Discord or download as an image to share with your alliance.

👋 New here? Here's how it works

1. Add players — scroll to Player Pool and add everyone who can drive the train. Set their rank (R5/R4/R3…) and which days they're available.
2. Set day rules — Day Rules lets you pick a strategy per day: rotation through officers, top tech contributor, random pick, or fill in manually (for MVP/Casino nights).
3. Apply Rules — click the green button to auto-fill the week. The same person won't be scheduled two days in a row.
4. Penalty Days — (bottom-right of the schedule) players who drove within this many days are skipped when randomizing, so the same person doesn't drive every week. Default is 7. Set to 0 to disable.
5. Post to Discord — connect an Alliance Account, add your Discord webhook, and post the schedule directly. Enable auto-post to have it send automatically every day.
